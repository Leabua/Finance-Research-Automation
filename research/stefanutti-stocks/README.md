# Stefanutti Stocks · JSE: SSK

Equity research on a South African construction and engineering group, focusing on restructuring, recurring earnings, cash conversion and the conversion of an order book into future activity.

## Excel model

The 11-sheet workbook includes standardised data, ratios, two order-book scenario sheets, an infrastructure-pipeline model, balance sheet, income statement, cash flows, closing prices, growth estimates and price targets.

The report examines the balance-sheet repair, the Kusile settlement and its effect on earnings quality, provisions, order-book coverage and cash conversion. It also includes the risks and assumptions behind the forward case.

## Report versions and sources

The main report is the later Markdown write-up dated **26 May 2026**, incorporating the FY2026 results. An [earlier research draft](archive/Stefanutti-earlier-research.md) and an [earlier data workbook](archive/SSK-earlier-data-workbook.xlsx) are retained in the archive.

The reviewed company results in `sources/` are a reference document, separate from the authored research in `reports/`. The saved CSV exports and workbook include different reporting periods; the source notes in the report explain the FY2026 additions.
