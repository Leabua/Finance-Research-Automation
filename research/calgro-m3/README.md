## Excel model

The 12 sheets cover standardised financial data; cumulative outlays; separate Bankenveld, Memorial Parks and other residential-development models; balance sheet; cash flow; closing prices; growth estimates; income statement; price targets; and recommendations.

The segment models separate the initial capital requirement from subsequent delivery, cash generation and the cumulative funding position. Their timing and margin assumptions are part of the original research scenario.
